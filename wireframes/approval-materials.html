Prepare internal approval

The decision is yours. The approval is not.

Materials for the case you carry to whoever holds the budget: each one dated, self-consistent, and open without a form. Forward them as they are, or build on the page they package.

Read Asterra Shield

Made to leave this site and still hold.

A business case is read by someone who was not in the room where it was assembled. Everything here is packaged for that reader: each artifact is self-contained, carries its date, and names its source next to the claim, so it can be checked without calling you back.

None of it sits behind a form. The argument these artifacts package already stands on the solution page, and gating the packaged version would only tax the person preparing the approval. Your email address is not the price of your own business case.

Two rules hold across the library. One canonical value per figure, wherever the figure appears: a number that drifts between documents reads as unreliability, and it is your standing that carries it upward. And a date on every artifact: an undated document is a stale one by default.

Each item forwards on its own.

The solution page argues. The artifacts document it.

Forward the solution page first: it carries the whole rationale, the problem, the regulations and the paper, and it survives being read without you. Attach the artifacts your chain will ask for next, the sheet for the budget line, the mapping for the audit question.

Then expect the chain to shape the conversation. CAPEX sits with the owner, an approval matrix decides who signs, and a frame agreement can decide whether a supplier may be bought from at all. The request form carries a role field so that this surfaces in the first conversation rather than in the third meeting.

The rationale is already on the solution page.

If the library gives you nothing for your case, the page it packages still argues it: dated, self-consistent, forwardable as it stands. An open question after that is worth a conversation.

Screen: Approval materials screen · state: base · flow node: approval (flows.md, MAIN JOB, approval branch) · job: R3, "Prepare business cases and recommendations" (jtbd.md §3), the core job of the primary persona since 2026-08-18; E2 as its quality constraint · entity: O6 Approval Artifact: the ungated library of forwardable, dated, self-consistent artifacts (sitemap.md §1, §5) · persona: P2 Fleet IT / IT Superintendent prepares, P4 Owner / CAPEX approver receives · states: empty, error, loading. No success: the approval branch models none, and a normally loaded library is the base file, not a success.

Reached from: ready "needs internal approval first" to approval; contextual level, entered from Solution detail (sitemap.md §6.3, raised DEEP to CONTEXTUAL 2026-08-18). Leads to: aRelevant "yes" to Inquiry. Failure exits: aError / aEmpty to aFallback "Use the forwardable solution page itself instead?" yes to Inquiry; refusing the fallback is deadStall and deliberately gets no button.

Zones

Head. The claim names the job: the buyer chose, the budget holder approves, so whatever this page shows will be retold upward (O6 definition). The ghost CTA points to the R3 primary carrier, the forwardable solution page: this library complements that page, it does not replace it (sitemap.md §6.4, the resolved [Г1] debt).

Why ungated. O6 is ungated by default: a gate adds friction with no job underneath (jtbd.md §8.3). The same section carries the two E2 rules: one canonical value per figure, a date on every artifact.

Artifact list. Structure real, contents honest. The forwardable summary and the compliance mapping resolve to existing pages; the specification sheet and the outcome case stay declared slots marked [TBD — no source]: a figure and a client for a fictional brand would be invented facts (sitemap.md O5 fillable-proof table). The accreditation overview names categories only; real companies are never named as Asterra's partners (brief §6.1).

No download controls. Download mechanics is a build-stage decision, so artifacts appear as rows and page links; nothing promises a file the wireframe cannot stand behind.

Fallback tail. aFallback made permanent: identical in all four states, offering the forwardable solution page and the expert conversation. Visible on base too, because the solution page is the job's primary carrier, not a consolation.

Grayscale, no brand colour, photography, icons, shadows, gradients, web fonts or motion. Copy is real domain content: no invented product, figure, certification or client.
