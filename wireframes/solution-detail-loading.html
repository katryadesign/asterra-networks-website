Rewritten in the review pass — headings now carry claims

The first version was headed by taxonomy labels ("Situations it answers", "Budget-grade facts", "Where it applies"): the IA showing through the page. Every section now leads with the claim its content makes ("The audit is where cyber fails first", "What it closes, and the paper you keep", "The facts for the budget file"), situations read as sentences, and the sector cross-link folded into the closing decision block instead of holding a section of its own. Same objects (O1 fields, O2 links, O5 attributes), same flow exits, different register. The head gained the brief §6 contextual inquiry CTA, which is mandated for solution pages.
