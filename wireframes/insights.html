Why the screen exists, quoted from sitemap.md §2: "REOPENED 2026-08-18 — the cut stands as written, but its basis was incomplete. The reference-site audit (research/scope-check.md §2.3) shows news items there are, in substance, partnership announcements — i.e. the carrier of O5 partner-accreditation freshness, not content marketing. What is still missing is buyer-side evidence that anyone reads it for that purpose. [...] Until then it stays out of the main list — reopened, not promoted."

What that means on the page. The screen ships as hygiene. The feed is restricted to the two item kinds the draft job names, regulation deadlines and accreditation freshness: no topics, no authors, no subscription block, no promotion into navigation.

No article pages. Every "Read more" is href="#". Article pages do not exist and will not be built: sitemap.md §2 keeps "Insights / News article" out of the main entity list.

Single source (E2). The first three items are the same three that stand in entry.html's Latest news, verbatim. The two added items stay in the same genre: one restates the MSC.428(98) annual-DOC recurrence already fixed on norm.html and entry.html, one records an accreditation at category level only (navigation and bridge manufacturers, brief §6.1). No invented figures, no named companies, no invented dates.
