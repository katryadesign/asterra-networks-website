Rebuilt after review — why the sections changed

The first version was headed by taxonomy labels ("Arrive from the situation you are in", "The regulations with a date on them", "The six areas, in fleet terms"): IA documentation formatted as a page. Competitor sector pages carry narrative sections, a claim, a paragraph, a route. The rebuild merges situations and regulations into one section whose rows read as sentences a superintendent would say, tying situation to norm to the area that answers it; features two offers with real copy; and keeps the remaining four areas as a compact list. Same objects (O4, O2, O1), same links, different register.

Head CTAs: the entry screen's "no quote CTA" rule is scoped to the entry screen (sitemap.md §5); industry pages may route to the expert (brief §6). Both CTAs lead to flow-backed destinations.

Crew section (S1) carries the retention framing and the MLC tie only; quotas and tariffs stay on the Crew access & control screen, outside this batch, so its link is inert. Proof is O5 with [TBD] slots per the fictional-brand rule.

flows.md draws iError → fallback; the clarification recorded in flows.md (2026-08-18) resolves the industry entrant to guided matching, per _conventions.md §5.2. No retry node exists for industry, so the error state offers no reload button.
