Solutions & Services · Crew connectivity

Give the crew what it asks for. Keep the rules yours.

Crew internet is decided as a set of rules: who gets how much and when, what a top-up costs, what is filtered, where access switches off, and what the office sees. The connection itself is the easy part. This page is about the rules, because the rules are what you answer for.

The altitude decision

flows.md gives R5 its defining decision: altitude "Does it speak policy, quotas, ranks, geo, costs, not bandwidth?" A bandwidth-led page is the documented trust collapse deadPitch, no recovery button: the market already sells these features deep in product pages, and repeating that is the failure this screen exists to avoid. Every section therefore states a rule, a cost dimension or an obligation; none leads with capacity, speed or coverage.
