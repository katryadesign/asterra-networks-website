Wireframe documentation — Guided matching screen

Screen: Guided matching screen · state: base · flow node: matching (flows.md, MAIN JOB) · job: MAIN, "confidently determine what exactly we need" (jtbd.md §2) · persona: P2 Fleet IT / IT Superintendent · states: empty, error, loading. No success: in the main flow success is confirmed only for Inquiry.

Reached from: needClear "no, the need is unclear" to matching. Leads to: mResult "yes" to Solution detail.

Zones

Interview (left, sticky). Three questions about the situation. sitemap.md §5 requires this screen to ask about the trigger, not the technology, so no question names a technology, a bandwidth tier or a product. Triggers are P2's documented arrival situations (personas.md P2 Context, plus newbuild ICT, standardisation and upgrade from the Anglo-Eastern posting) together with P1's docking, PSC and budget-line entries. The interview is permanent: it appears unchanged in all four states, which is what makes re-running the match possible without a separate control.

Result region (right). The only zone that differs between states. Base shows a ranked match with the reason it fits, what the buyer is left holding, and the route to Solution detail.

Fallback. fallback "yes, by industry" to Industry screen. Kept visible in every state, not only in the failure states, because recognition is a confirmed MAIN mechanism in its own right and not a consolation prize.

No filters here

This screen is an interview, not a faceted search. research.md §9.3 names the expert facet catalogue as the mechanism not to port: with six curated areas there is nothing to filter, and facets would expose exactly the cross-domain vocabulary that defeats the buyer. Loosening the criteria therefore means answering the situation question differently, which the permanent interview already allows.
