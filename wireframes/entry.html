Our position

We do not launch the satellites or build the bridge hardware. We are the party accredited to carry them, and the party you call when they stop.

Which is why the proof on this site is never a claim about ourselves. It is whose capacity we are authorised to resell, whose equipment we are approved to survey, and which dated requirement each system closes.

Wireframe documentation — Entry screen

Screen: Entry screen · state: base (stateless) · flow node: entry · job: MAIN (jtbd.md §2) · persona: P2 Fleet IT / IT Superintendent.

Composition pass — what changed and why

Section shape now varies. Every section previously used the same heading-left / content-right split, which reads as a stack rather than a page. The page now moves between a full-width hero, a single oversized statement, a split diagram, an asymmetric index, a dense list, an offset column and a three-part band.

Repeated eyebrows removed. A small uppercase label sat above almost every section. One deliberate kicker is voice; an eyebrow on every section is scaffolding. The headings carry the sections now.

Side-stripe accents removed. Maritime and the lead cells carried a thick left border as an accent device. Emphasis now comes from scale and column span.

Six areas are no longer six identical cards. The two areas that close a dated requirement take a two-column major row; the remaining four sit in a compact minor row. The hierarchy states which areas carry a deadline, which is the differentiating claim.

Copy pass. Em dashes, "cutting edge" and similar filler removed. Sentences name a specific thing the company does.

Still a wireframe

Grayscale only. No brand colour, photography, icons, shadows, gradients, web fonts or motion. Grey bands stand in for the dark-first treatment of brief §9 and are not a colour decision. Type is a system stack at a committed scale; the typeface itself is a design-stage choice.

Still absent, deliberately

No stat band and no named client case: both would be invented facts for a fictional brand (sitemap.md O5). No crew quota or tariff content, that vocabulary belongs to the Crew access screen. No quote CTA in the body.
