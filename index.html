Context

What the site has to do

The brief in four lines; the full version lives in CLAUDE.md.

The job

Turn a complex catalog of technologies and services into a clear digital experience: let a professional buyer quickly find the solution for their industry, understand its value, and reach an expert.

Primary conversion

A qualified inquiry sent from a Solution or Service page: "Send an Inquiry" / "Talk to an Expert". Deliberately no "Request a Quote" CTA on the homepage, and no multi-step quote wizard.

Primary audience

Maritime shipowners, fleet operators and technical managers lead the narrative. Enterprise IT/security, government, energy, transportation, research and emergency response are fully served but do not lead.

Direction

Editorial tech, dark-first, English only, expressive motion that respects prefers-reduced-motion. Desktop-first for B2B, with a fully realised responsive mobile experience.

How to read this

The evidence rule

The case study is built without access to real buyers, and it says so everywhere instead of hiding it. Every claim in the research layer carries one of these labels, so a reader can tell a read source from a guess at a glance.

Fact

A primary source was opened and read: a vacancy, a regulation, a classification requirement.

2nd-hand

Taken from a paraphrase because the primary source would not open. Quoted wording may not be verbatim.

Survey

Industry research with a published sample.

Audit

Recorded on a competitor's site by us, with a screenshot or URL.

Inferred

Our conclusion from the facts above, not an observation.

[?]

No data. Framed as a hypothesis, never dressed up as a finding.

The biggest hypothesis sits under the whole thing: that this buying work happens on a supplier's website at all. No public layer of buyer reviews exists in this category: the end user is loud online, the buyer is silent. That is a property of the market, not a failure of searching, and it is recorded as an open question.
